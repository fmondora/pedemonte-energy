Two things in the kitchen ask for a little extra care. Nothing else.

## The Kay knives — by hand, not in the dishwasher

The knives on the **magnetic bar** are **Kay knives**: the handle is **fine wood**.
They must be **washed by hand**. The dishwasher, with its heat and detergents, ruins
the wood within a few cycles.

![The knives on the magnetic bar](../assets/coltelli.jpg)

![All the knives](../assets/coltelli-kay.jpg)

## The pots

Some pots **don't work on induction**. There's no elegant way to tell them apart by eye:
try them, and if one doesn't heat, move to the next. Nothing breaks.

![The pots](../assets/pentole.jpg)

We also have some **lavec**, the **soapstone** pots from Valmalenco. They are local
objects, ancient as a technique: cook in them if you like — but treat them gently,
the stone doesn't like sudden temperature changes.
