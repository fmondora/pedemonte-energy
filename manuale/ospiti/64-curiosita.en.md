## Sleep in Europe, look at Africa

From the window, looking **south**, you see a mountain that geologically **isn't
European**. The **Insubric Line** (or Periadriatic Seam) runs right in front of the
house — the suture between the European and the Adriatic-African plates. Around here some
people **sleep in Europe and have lunch in Africa**.

## Shakespeare, perhaps

The story goes that **William Shakespeare** was conceived in **Tresivio**, in the Othello
house. Today you'll find the **Jom Bar** there.

## The UFOs of Valmalenco

In **Valmalenco**, they say, you can see **UFOs** — if you're lucky. There are lovely
hikes either way.

## The lazy marmots

At **Chiareggio** there are **domestic marmots**, and rather lazy ones.

## The Gaudí of Grosio

**Nicola di Cesare** lives in **Grosio** and has been building his Gaudí-style garden for
over thirty years. Free entry.
