Casa Pedemonte is a family house, not a holiday let. That means you'll find our things
around, and that's how it should be: use them.

It unfolds over several levels, from the **garage** at the bottom up to the **rooftop**
with the tub. In between, the **kitchen** with the dining room, the **bedrooms**, and a
garden that comes in through every window.

## The salt lamps

There is a **salt lamp on every floor**. We keep them **on, night and day** — it's not
an oversight. Please leave them as they are.

## The light

The house is designed to take light all day long. Francesco has asthma and needs large,
bright spaces: if you open the windows and let the air run through, you're doing exactly
what we do.

## How we live it

We eat together, and we consider lunch and dinner moments to be together, not just to
feed ourselves. We are vegetarian (seafood aside) and prefer healthy food, ideally from
the garden. None of this is required of you — but if you feel like living at that pace,
the house is built for it.
