## The goldfish

There's a **pond with goldfish**. **You don't need to feed them.** The only thing:
every now and then **check the pond drain**, so the water can flow out. If it clogs,
the water stagnates.

![The fish pond](../assets/pesci-rossi.jpg)

## Paola, Ciaparat, Shanti and Maya

They are our **four cats**. They all live **indoors and outdoors**, as they please, and
the same few rules apply to all of them.

- **Feed them when they need it**, at least **twice a day**. Inside or outside the
  house, whichever suits you.
- **If you go out and the house is empty, leave them outside.**
- **At night they are perfectly fine outdoors.** It's their normal, not neglect.

Don't worry if you don't see them all at once: they come and go as they like, and it's
not unusual for one to vanish for half a day.
