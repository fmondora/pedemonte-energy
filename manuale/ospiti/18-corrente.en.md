you reset it here.

The house has a **solar** system with battery storage, so much of the energy you use
comes from the roof. You don't need to think about it — but if you happen to cook or run
the washing machine **in the middle of the day**, you're using the sun instead of the
grid, and that's the thing that pleases us most.
