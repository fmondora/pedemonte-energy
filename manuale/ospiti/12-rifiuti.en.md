Collection is door-to-door. The rule is simple: **put the bag out on the street after
7am** on the right day, near the main road. They come round during the morning.

The Berbenno collection **calendar** is the municipal one. Even if it suggests **blue,
yellow or black** bags, **it isn't mandatory** to use that colour: what matters is the day.

## Plastic

We keep it **outside**, together with the glass bin. There's a **wooden box with the
bags** inside.

<video src="../assets/rifiuti-plastica-vetro.mp4" controls playsinline></video>

## Paper

We collect it **in the garage**. Same treatment: out in the morning, on the right day.

## Glass

Glass **doesn't go on the street**: take it to the **big green containers** in the
village. There are **two spots**; the handiest is **next to the playground**.
