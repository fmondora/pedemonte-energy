**via Pedemonte 425**
**23010 Berbenno di Valtellina** — Sondrio, Italy

We're on the **Rhaetian** side, above the village: the road climbs, and the house is on
the right. The **garage** is at the lowest level; from there you enter the house by the
**internal staircase**.

## Where to put the car

The garage fits **two cars**, but **one of ours is almost always there**: count on
**one space**. If you come in two cars, the second one is fine outside.

## First thing to do

Nothing in particular. The **salt lamps** are already on and stay that way; the **wi-fi**
is called **Akasha** and you'll find the password in the **Wi-fi and technology** section.
