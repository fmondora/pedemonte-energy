## Right nearby

- **The Masino river** at Ardenno, **10 minutes by bike**. It's where the whole village
  cools off in summer. Four spots: **la cascata** (the waterfall, the most
  adrenaline-fuelled, full of teenagers), **le pozze** (the pools, warmer water, great
  with small children), **il chiosco** (the kiosk, sandwiches and picnics, 400 m from the
  river) and **gli scivoli** (the slides, quiet and beautiful).
- **Sentiero dei Terrazzamenti** — starts practically at the house.
- **Piramidi di Postalesio** — 15 minutes by car, 50 by bike. Our little Cappadocia,
  minus the balloons.

## By bike

- **Short loop**, 40 minutes: up to **Monastero** and back, passing a
  **hundred-year-old chestnut tree**.
- **Sentiero Valtellina** to **Sondrio**, two hours round trip, with ice cream.
- **Colico and the lake**: take the Sentiero Valtellina and keep going. Four hours round
  trip, thoroughly pleasant.

## Hikes

- **Ponte nel Cielo**, Val Tartano — 30 minutes by car. A Tibetan bridge; the **ring of
  the bridges** takes about 4 hours.
- **Val di Mello** — 30 minutes. Granite and meadows, easy walking, and you can swim at
  the **Bidet della Contessa**. Leave the car at San Martino, then on foot.
- **Lago Palù** — from San Giuseppe / I Barchi. Very easy; great with a sledge in winter.
- **Laghetti di Campagneda and Alpe Prabello** — an hour's drive, two hours' walk. Eat at
  the refuge, swim in the lakes, and you're on the Swiss border: don't be alarmed if your
  phone welcomes you to Switzerland.
- **Rifugio Marinelli** — an hour by car and 2.5 hours' walk, best started early. You see
  Bernina and Roseg.
- **Ventina glaciological trail** — the glacier and the marks of its retreat. Beautiful
  and melancholy at once.
- **Tour of the Four Lakes**, Valgerola — in summer. Car to Pescegallo, 40 minutes.
- **Pizzo della Rosetta** — from Bar Bianco, a 2.5-hour loop. A real summit but an easy
  one: good if you're starting out.

## Sport

- **Rafting** with **Indomita** at Castione, 7 minutes from home. About 4 hours.
- **Windsurfing** on **Lake Como**, at **Soul Surfer**: Luisa is happy to give lessons.
- **Horse riding**: **Isola del Cavallo**, 20 minutes — Raimondo, +39 392 329 5853. For
  private lessons, **Sergio Abram**, +39 333 341 9758 (speaks French and English).
- **Bouldering**: in the parents' room there's a **Domino Cassin crash pad**, take it to
  **Valmasino**. You'll need climbing shoes — if you're a size 40 or 44, ask us. In Anna's
  room, on the first floor, there are many books about climbing.

## Museums and towns

- **La Bagnada mine** — 40 minutes. An old talc mine, you walk through the tunnels. Even
  in summer: bring a windbreaker.
- **Chiavenna** — historic centre, Piuro butter biscuits, **violino di capra** (a Slow
  Food presidium). Nearby, the **Acquafraggia** falls and **Palazzo Vertemate** (guided
  tours by reservation).
- **Grosio rock carvings** — free entry. Next to it, the **garden of Nicola di Cesare**,
  the local "Gaudí".
