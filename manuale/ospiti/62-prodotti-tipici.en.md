## To eat

- **Pizzoccheri** — buckwheat tagliatelle with cabbage, potatoes and melted cheese.
- **Polenta** — in many versions and with many toppings.
- **Taroz** — crushed green beans and potatoes, with butter, bacon and cheese.
- **Bisciola** — a cake of rye flour, dried figs, chestnuts, walnuts and butter.
- **Cupeta** — a dessert of walnuts and honey.
- **Violino di capra** — a Slow Food presidium, found in Chiavenna. You carve it holding
  it like a violin: hence the name.

![Violino di capra](../assets/violino-di-capra.jpg)

- **Bitto storico** — there's a cheese cellar in Valgerola. To visit it or decorate your
  own wheel, call **Carlo**, +39 335 161 9279, and say you're staying at our house.
- **Alpine ricotta and butter** from **La Taida**, 2 km from the house. In summer they
  take the cows up to the alpine pasture: drive to Tartano, leave the car near **Albergo
  Vallunga** and walk up towards **Alpe Sona**.

## To wear

- **Pedui** — the slippers of Valmalenco.

## For the table and the kitchen

- **Pezzotti** — hand-woven rugs.
- **Lavec** — soapstone pots from Valmalenco. We have a few in the house.
