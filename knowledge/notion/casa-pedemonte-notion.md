=== PAGINA: About us ===
We desire to be a conscious and respective family towards everything that surrounds us: we see the Earth as a sacred place hosting us in her arms, like a mother with her child, like a sister with her sister, like a brother with his brother. We feel a brotherhood and Sisterhood relationship with our Earth and everything/everyone hosted on it.
We are vegetarian (with the exception of seafood), and we prefer healthy, biodynamic self-grown food.

#### **Francesco, 1974**
He is a creative and sensible person, in love with life. He likes balancing working life with relaxation moments at home. He also enjoys sharing moments with friends. He needs sacred spaces where he can stay with himself when meditating, relaxing, or sunbathing. Furthermore, he loves water and swimming, surrounded by nature.
Francesco suffers from asthma is needs large spaces with great light.

#### **Lucia, 1977**
She loves cats, nature, gardening, and cooking foods grown in her and the family's vegetable garden with/for friends (sisters and brothers) because she thinks self-grown foods help you connect with nature and cook with them creating magical moments. She also likes traveling, organizing home swaps worldwide, knowing new places and visiting historical and natural areas. She doesn’t enjoy living in big cities…too much chaos! but still, she recognizes the utility of the services that big cities offer, so she prefers having a big town near the place she lives in.
It is not told, but Lucia in the future would love to be the “italian cook” in town. Having guests at her space or being her at their place is just a flavor.

#### **Vicki, 2010**
She loves to swim (rather in a swimming pool in the garden house than in the sea) and to relax in the sun. She also loves having ‘her spaces’ where she can create things: objects, paints, origami. Relaxing near a Tv or playing on the Nintendo switch is her dream life. She also loves hammocks, cats and dogs and playng arpa.

#### **Anna, 2008**
She is a very active person, and she loves every kind of sport, “mind, body, spirit.” She also loves every type of music, that’s why she’s playing the piano. She likes sharing moments with friends and going out with them, this means that public transports and cycle paths are very useful to her. She also enjoys studying and visiting museums/historical places.

### **Our values**
We try to be very inclusive. We are in a community all around the world of friends with whom we share thoughts, values, lifestyles, and houses to stay together despite a possible physical distance.
These are some of our central values:
- Our life is on One Earth
- Like love, everything is abundant
- No one is left behind
- We are all Brothers and Sisters
- Our life is Sacred, even the Earth
- Regenerate spaces with life
- Be conscious and act ethically
- Disconnection is connection, connected but disconnected
- I, We, Plus One
We think that what is us, is also of our friends and people which we respect. When we travel, mostly we home swap. Just because, sharing is caring.

### **Our connection to nature**
We inhabit everything around us-it is nourishment that happens not only with food but also with music, scents, spaces and the landscape. Nature is our outer body; home is where we take it. We live a lot outdoor, and we like to think of the garden as coming into the house. Just as life has its cyclicities, the living space must also introduce circularities. Water, ponds, and springs are elements of circularity. In outdoor areas, we like to have a vegetable garden and fruit plants where we can think about harvesting all our vegetables. We love harmony with nature in materials.

### **Lifestyle and mindset**
What life would we want to live if we were on its last day? Life is full of love and abundance. A time when we were able to bring the world into our life and life into our world. Our passage will have been one in which what we have done will endure through time. If not taken care of by others, nature will be the one who can take it all back. We try to project our thinking with a long-term view: when we think of a house, we imagine it at 300 years. That is why we feel it necessary to use only elements from nature, such as stones and wood. We like to think that we are custodians of this land, to give meaning to this existence of ours even with the artifacts we leave behind. We think of them as sacred; we consider our passage blessed and magnificent. Everything comes to us so shining and abundant.
This is why we need places where nature predominates, where we can host friends and passersby. We like to coexist with the joy and love that this life offers us.
We do not like new construction or over-urbanized countries. We like the idea of taking up something that has been abandoned and regenerating it rather than building something new. Even if we have to tear down the property and rebuild it again, we like to implement a regenerative strategy. We want, in our transition, to enhance something that is abandoned and "has little value."
We want to be mostly self-sufficient. Disconnection from the world, is connection. Thinking about houses, we love the principles of off-grid houses or eco-villages.
We are ecologists and believe that it goes beyond the conservation of environment. We like to think of an ecology of the mind, relationships and love.

#### Abundance and interdependence
We want to maintain a conscious attitude and think it is right to think about "plus one."  The "plus one" is the meaning we are giving to abundance. In our lifestyle, in every activity, we focus on how that activity can benefit an additional stakeholder besides us.
So at lunch, we always set the table for an extra meal and try to eat for 75 percent of what is on the table.
So even at home, we like to think of spaces as "plus ones." We like to think of a space for guests of various kinds to come in and out of the house freely. That's why having a small outbuilding devoted to "plus ones" might be a good idea.
We are not binding, we also like to involve the people who work with us to think in this logic of abundance.
In the future, when we are not in that house, we like that house to be lived in by others:
- home exchange is one solution we practice;
- temporarily renting the main house and donating the temporary use of the shared space "plus one"

#### **The type of house**
Wind and type of sea. We like to live where the sea creates a basin and the waves are not too big. We dream of a house of 100-120 square meters and to an outbuilding of 30 square meters and a garage of 30 square meters.
Our houses are small spas. We consider ourselves guests in our places and we like the house to smell like its own. Surrounded by greenery. Close to the sea, in a place with little wind and potentially in a basin.
We need 3 double bedrooms at least, a large living room with a large kitchen. Lucia loves to cook. The house needs to be very bright and receive light all day long.
We like to think of a water source where we can create a biolake.
Even rainwater, we want to collect it and use it to irrigate the garden or orchard or feed the biolake.
In addition, we need a garage, where we install a charging station to recharge the car and where we keep bicycles and atraments.
A view of the ocean is in our dreams.

### Dreaming for a future day there
This is a typical day life, we usually ‘reproduce’ in every place we go :) We like mornings: we wake up early to dedicate some time to ourselves meditating or taking yoga sessions, in a peaceful and quiet surroundings.
After that we work remotely for some hours, also outside in the garden, if the weather is not too hot or cold, while girls are at school.
We like having lunches/dinners together and we consider them moments to share thoughts and stay together.Iin the afternoon we alternate work/study to the sea or sport or gardening or visiting places, spending also some time with friends especially for dinner/evening at home.

#### Swim and refreshing
- Masino  ([Location](https://goo.gl/maps/qSK8TMTidpWD5dNY7)) 10 mins bike→ Our dauthers love hanging out with their friends, but in summer is too hot to just walk around the neighbourhood. We discovered a river, in Ardenno, it’s only 7km away and it’s the most refreshing and fun place to be with friends. It’s called the Masino River, it’s very long, in Ardenno there are four places which are very famous: “la cascata” (water fall) “le pozze” (water pools) “il chiosco” (kiosk) and “gli sciovoli” (the slides). La cascata is the most famous one for people my age, I recommend it to make new friends, in fact there you’ll find many young teens because the place is very adrenaline-pumping and some times a bit dangerous.  La pozza is instead famous for families, especially ones with little kids, it’s not dangerous and the ‘pools’ are a bit warmer than the waterfall.
If you want to have a picnic near the river, the kiosk is the place for you. There is a kiosk just a few meters away from the river (400meters I believe): you can order sandwiches and eat them on “La plage” of the river. Gli scivoli isn’t a very famous place, but it’s beautiful and peaceful.
